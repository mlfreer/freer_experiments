{% include '_static/global/GeneralInstructions.html' %} Structure of a Round
  • At the beginning of a round, you will see all the possible alternatives on your screen.
  • Each alternative is either open or closed.
  • If an alternative is open, then it appears face up. You can observe both the high and low payments for the alternative.
  • If an alternative is closed, then it appears face down. You can observe neither the high nor the low payments for the alternative.

Structure of a Round
  • For each alternative, there are four buttons that you can press:
    1. Show
    2. Discard
    3. Select
    4. Confirm

Buttons
  • At the beginning of a round, all alternatives are closed.
  • If you press the Show button, then it opens the alternative. You are only allowed to have at most two open alternatives at any time.
  • If you press the Discard button, then it permanently removes the alternative.
  • If you press the Select button, then it (1) tentatively selects the alternative as your chosen alternative and (2) discards any other open alternative.
  • If you press the Confirm button, then it finalises the alternative as your chosen alternative. This button ends the round.

How Final Earnings are Calculated
  • Your final earnings in the experiment are the sum of the following items:
    • {{showup}} show-up payment
    • earnings from 1 randomly selected round
  • You will be paid after the end of the experiment.
