Welcome back to part 2 of our study. Below, you see the same consent form as was presented to you during part 1 of the study. To continue, please confirm your consent.



Invitation to our study
This experiment is conducted by researchers from the Department of Economics at the University of Essex and Free University of Amsterdam. Should you have any questions, you can raise them with the Principal Investigator (contact details below).

Background on the project
We are conducting a series of experiments on decision-making.

Experiment
The experiment consists of two parts. You will receive {{ session.config.participation_fee }} for showing up on time. During the experiment, you can earn further rewards. How much you earn depends on your decisions, the decisions of others, and random chance. Detailed instructions will be provided to you on your computer screen before each part begins. At the end of the experiment, one of the rounds in this session will be randomly selected. You will receive the reward you earned in that round. All your answers and decisions will be treated confidentially.

Informed consent
Should you agree to take part in this experiment, you will be asked to sign a consent form before the experiment commences.

Withdrawal
Your participation is voluntary and you will be free to withdraw from the project at any time up to the end of the experimental session without giving any reason and without penalty. If you wish to withdraw during the experiment, you simply need to raise your hand, let us know and you will be able to leave immediately. Any data that have already been collected will be destroyed in this case unless you inform us that you are happy for us to use such data for the scientific purposes of the project. Note that once the experimental session has finished it will not be possible to withdraw your data anymore as all the data are gathered anonymously so your data cannot be identified in order to extract it.

Data gathered
We will collect only the data which you input in the lab experiment, i.e., your answers to the questions we ask. The anonymous data will be analysed and used to write the study reports and academic articles. During the project only the Principal Investigator (Dr Mikhail Freer) and supervisors will have access to the data. Anonymised data will be deposited in the UK Data Archive upon completion of the project and will be available for researchers to use in the future. Signed consent forms will be kept separately from individual experimental data and locked in a drawer until the end of the project. Only the research team as well as the Essex Lab manager (Dr Patrick Lown), will have access to the consent forms. For questions about data management, please contact the Principal Investigator (Dr Mikhail Freer m.freer@essex.ac.uk) or the Data Protection Officer (dpo@essex.ac.uk).

Findings
After the end of the project, we will publish the findings of our experiments (all data published will be anonymised). We will be happy to provide you with a lay summary of the main findings and with copies of the articles published if you express an interest.

Concerns and complaints
If you have any concerns about any aspect of the study or you have a complaint, in the first instance please contact the principal investigator of the project. If are still concerned or you think your complaint has not been addressed to your satisfaction, please contact the Head of Department in the principal investigator’s department. If you are still not satisfied, please contact the University’s Research Governance and Planning Manager.

Ethical approval
This project has been reviewed by the Social Science Faculty Ethics Sub-Committee on behalf of the University of Essex Ethics Committee and has been given approval (Approval number: ETH2425-0847).
