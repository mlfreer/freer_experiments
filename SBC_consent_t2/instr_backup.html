Lottery ticket

In this study, you evaluate a lottery ticket. The lottery ticket consists of two lotteries with different monetary amounts that you can receive as part of your bonus payment. Each lottery has two possible outcomes that are equally likely. What outcomes will be paid out to you will be determined by the flip of two independent virtual coins.

  • Lottery 1: Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the first coin flip results in heads, and £{{ participant.x_draw }} if it results in tails.
  • Lottery 2: Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the second coin flip results in heads, and £0 if it results in tails.

Your lottery ticket earnings are given by the sum of payments from the two lotteries. For example, if the first coin flip is tails and the second is heads, your lottery ticket earnings are £{{ participant.x_draw }} + £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} = £{{ example_sum }}. As another example, if the first coin flip is heads and the second is tails, your lottery ticket earnings are £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} + £0 = £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}.

When do you learn more about your lotteries?
  • Next, you will learn the outcome of Lottery 1. This means that you will know whether Lottery 1 pays you £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} or £{{ participant.x_draw }}. However, you will not learn the outcome of Lottery 2 until the end of today's part.
  • Afterwards, you may make additional decisions.
  • At the end of this study today, you will learn the result of Lottery 2 and your bonus payment for the participation of this study.

Payment

To be able to purchase the lottery ticket, you will receive a budget of £{{ C.ENDOWMENT }} in this study. At the end of part 2 of the study, any payments for purchasing {% if participant.treatment == 2 %} the option and {% endif %} the lottery ticket will be subtracted from that budget. You will receive the remaining budget as a bonus payment. This means that your total bonus payment in this study will consist of:

Bonus Payment = Budget - Lottery ticket payment + Lottery earnings


For example, if you purchase the lottery ticket for £6 and receive earnings of £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}, your bonus payment will be

£{{ C.ENDOWMENT }} - £6 + £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} = £17

Random selection

Today, at the end of the study, the computer will randomly select one of the questions, with each question being equally likely to be chosen. If you chose to purchase the lottery ticket {% if participant.treatment == 3 %} (and not to return it) {% endif %} in that question, you will receive the lottery ticket, but your budget of £{{ C.ENDOWMENT }} will be reduced by that price. Otherwise, you will not receive the lottery ticket. You will then not receive any earnings from the lottery ticket, but you will keep your budget.

25% of participants in this study will be randomly selected for the bonus payment and will receive the bonus payment within two weeks of the completion of the study. For 98% of participants, we will implement exactly their decisions. For the remaining 2% of participants, you will be paid and make decisions as if you purchased {% if participant.treatment == 2 %} the option to buy {% endif %} the lottery ticket. {% if participant.treatment == 2 or participant.treatment == 3 %} That means that all participants will make decisions in today's part 2, because it may be that the computer randomly selects them to make a purchase, and everyone's decisions may potentially involve receiving a lottery ticket.{% endif %}
