Lottery ticket:

In this study, you evaluate a lottery ticket. The lottery ticket consists of two lotteries with different monetary amounts. Each lottery has two possible outcomes that are equally likely. What outcomes will be paid out to you will be determined by the flip of two independent virtual coins.

  • Lottery 1: Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the first coin flip results in heads, and £{{ participant.x_draw }} if it results in tails.
  • Lottery 2: Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the second coin flip results in heads, and £0 if it results in tails.

Your earnings from the lottery ticket are given by the sum of payments from the two lotteries. For example, if the first coin flip is tails and the second is heads, you will earn £{{ participant.x_draw }} + £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} = £{{ example_sum }}. As another example, if the first coin flip is heads and the second is tails, you will earn £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} + £0 = £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}.

When do you learn more about your lotteries?

  • Next, you will learn the outcome of Lottery 1. This means that you will know whether Lottery 1 pays you £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} or £{{ participant.x_draw }}. However, you will not learn the outcome of Lottery 2 until the end of today's part.
  • Afterwards, you may make additional decisions.
  • At the end of this study today, you will learn the result of Lottery 2 and your total payoff for the participation of this study.

Your total payment consists of the payment for the option in part 1 and the payment for the lottery ticket in part 2 (if you choose to buy the option and/or the purchase the lottery ticket).
