In this study, you evaluate a lottery ticket consisting of two lotteries with different monetary amounts you can receive as part of your bonus payment. Each lottery has two equally likely outcomes, determined by the flip of two independent virtual coins.

1
Lottery 1 Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the first coin is heads, and £{{ participant.x_draw }} if tails.
2
Lottery 2 Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the second coin is heads, and £0 if tails.

Your lottery ticket earnings are the sum of payments from both lotteries.

Example 1: First coin tails, second heads → £{{ participant.x_draw }} + £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} = £{{ example_sum }}
Example 2: First coin heads, second tails → £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} + £0 = £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}

At the end of Part 2, any payments for purchasing {% if participant.treatment == 2 %} the option and {% endif %} the lottery ticket will be subtracted from that budget. Bonus Payment = Budget − Lottery ticket payment + Lottery earnings

Example: If you purchase the lottery ticket for £6 and receive earnings of £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}, your bonus payment will be:

{{ C.ENDOWMENT }} − £6 + £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} = £17
