Guidelines
  • The amount of money that you ultimately earn in the experiment depends on your decisions and random chance.
  • You have each earned a {{showup}} payment for showing up on time.

Structure of the Experiment
  • The main part of the experiment consists of 20 rounds.
  • 1 of the 20 rounds will be randomly selected to determine your payment.
  • Each round is equally likely to be selected.
  • Thus, it is important for you to take each round seriously.
  • We will also ask you to complete a brief survey at the end of the experiment.

Structure of a Round
  • In each round, you will make a choice among several possible alternatives.
  • The number of possible alternatives will range from 2 to 12.
  • Each alternative is a lottery with a 50% chance of a high payment and a 50% chance of a low payment.
  • The minimum value for the low payment is £2 and the maximum value for the high payment is £30.
  • For some alternatives, the high and low payments are the same.
