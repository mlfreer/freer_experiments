Examples

On this page, please work through the following examples that are designed to familiarize yourself with the decisions you will face in this study. You can only proceed to the study if you answer all questions correctly.


1. Imagine that, in part 2, the coin flip for lottery 1 results in heads and also the coin flip for lottery 2 results in heads. Imagine further that you decide not to purchase the lottery ticket at a price of 6 today, and that decision is randomly selected to be paid out to you. What is your total payment at the end of part 2 of this study?


2. As above, imagine that, in part 2, the coin flip for lottery 1 results in heads and also the coinflip for lottery 2 results in heads. However, now imagine that you decide to purchase the lottery ticket at a price of 10 today, and that decision is randomly selected to be paid out to you. What is your total payment at the end of part 2 of this study?


2. As above, imagine that, in part 2, the coin flip for lottery 1 results in heads and also the coinflip for lottery 2 results in tails. However, now imagine that you decide to purchase the lottery ticket at a price of 6 today, and that decision is randomly selected to be paid out to you. What is your total payment at the end of part 2 of this study?
