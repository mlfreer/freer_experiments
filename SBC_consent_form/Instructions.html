Today, you will evaluate a lottery ticket consisting of two lotteries. Each has two equally likely outcomes, determined by two independent virtual coin flips.

1
Lottery 1 Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the first coin is heads, and £{{ participant.x_draw }} if it is tails.
2
Lottery 2 Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the second coin is heads, and £0 if it is tails.

Your lottery ticket earnings equal the sum of both lottery outcomes. For example:

  • First coin tails, second coin tails → £{{ participant.x_draw }} + £0 = £{{ participant.x_draw }}
  • First coin heads, second coin tails → £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} + £0 = £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}

Today, you can buy the right to purchase the lottery ticket next week at a fixed purchase price. This right is called an option. The option has no benefit other than giving you the right to purchase the lottery ticket in Part 2.

You will see various combinations of option prices and purchase prices. For each combination, you will make two decisions across the two parts:

1
Part 1 (today): Buy or decline the option.
2
Part 2: Only if you bought the option, you decide whether to purchase the lottery ticket at the pre-specified purchase price. If you did not buy the option today, you cannot purchase the corresponding lottery ticket in Part 2.

In Part 2 you will first learn Lottery 1's outcome (£{{ session.config.A_BAR }} or £{{ participant.x_draw }}), then decide on the ticket — but before learning Lottery 2's outcome.

📌 Note: If you do not buy the option today, you cannot purchase the lottery ticket in Part 2. Your total payment covers both today's and Part 2's decisions.
{% endif %} {% if participant.treatment == 3 %}

Today, you can purchase the lottery ticket.

Your ticket includes a refund option that you can use in Part 2. Today, you will see various combinations of purchase prices for the lottery ticket and possible refund amounts.

In Part 2 you will first learn Lottery 1's outcome (£{{ session.config.A_BAR }} or £{{ participant.x_draw }}). For each combination of lottery price and refund amount, you can then either keep your lottery ticket or return it for a pre-specified refund. You will make that decision after learning Lottery 1's outcome but before learning Lottery 2's outcome.

→
If you keep the lottery ticket (so reject the refund), you receive the lottery ticket earnings. However, you have then already paid the lottery ticket purchase price.
→
If you use the refund (so return the lottery ticket), you receive the refund amount back, but you do not receive any lottery ticket earnings. However, you have then already paid the lottery ticket purchase price.
📌 Note: If you do not purchase the lottery ticket today, you cannot purchase the lottery ticket in Part 2 and you cannot use the refund option for that lottery ticket.
