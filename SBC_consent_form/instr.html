Today, you will evaluate a lottery ticket consisting of two lotteries. Each has two equally likely outcomes, determined by independent virtual coin flips.

1
Lottery 1 Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the first coin is heads, and £{{ participant.x_draw }} if it is tails.
2
Lottery 2 Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the second coin is heads, and £0 if it is tails.

Your lottery ticket earnings equal the sum of both lottery outcomes. For example:

  • First coin tails, second coin tails → £{{ participant.x_draw }}
  • First coin heads, second coin tails → £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} + £0 = £{{ session.config.A_BAR }}

⏰ When do you learn the outcomes?

→
Part 2 (start): You learn Lottery 1's result — whether it pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} or £{{ participant.x_draw }}. You will not yet know Lottery 2's result.
→
You may then make additional decisions.
✓
Part 2 (end): You learn Lottery 2's result and your final bonus payment.

Today, you can buy the right to purchase the lottery ticket next week at a fixed price. This right is called an option. The option has no benefit other than giving you the right to purchase later.

You will make two decisions across the two parts:

1
Part 1 (today): Buy or decline the option to purchase the lottery ticket.
2
Part 2: Only if you bought the option, decide whether to purchase the lottery ticket at the pre-specified price. You cannot purchase if you skipped the option.

In Part 2 you will first learn Lottery 1's outcome (£{{ session.config.A_BAR }} or £{{ participant.x_draw }}), then decide on the ticket — but before learning Lottery 2's outcome.

📌 Note: If you do not buy the option today, you cannot purchase the lottery ticket in Part 2. Your total payment covers both today's and Part 2's decisions.
{% endif %} {% if participant.treatment == 3 %}

📌 Note: If you do not purchase today, you cannot purchase in Part 2 and cannot use the refund option. Your payment covers both parts.
