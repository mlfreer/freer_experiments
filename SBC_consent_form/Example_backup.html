On this page, please work through the following examples that are designed to familiarize yourself with the decisions you will face in this study. You can only proceed to the study if you answer all questions correctly.

All questions refer to the lottery ticket that you will evaluate in the study:
  • Lottery 1: Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the first coin flip results in heads, and £{{ participant.x_draw }} if it results in tails.
  • Lottery 2: Pays £{{ session.config.A_BAR }} if the second coin flip results in heads, and £0 if it results in tails.

{% if participant.treatment == 0 %} 1. Imagine that you decide not to purchase the lottery ticket at a price of £6 today, and that decision is randomly selected to be paid out to you. Further, imagine that, in part 2, the coin flip for lottery 1 results in heads and the coin flip for lottery 2 results in heads. What would be your bonus payment at the end of part 2 of this study?


2. Now, imagine that you decide to purchase the lottery ticket at a price of £10 today. As above, further imagine that, in part 2, the coin flip for lottery 1 results in heads and the coin flip for lottery 2 results in heads. What would be your bonus payment at the end of part 2 of this study?


2. Now, imagine that you decide to purchase the lottery ticket at a price of £10 today. Differently, now imagine that, in part 2, the coin flip for lottery 1 results in heads and the coin flip for lottery 2 results in tails. What would be your bonus payment at the end of part 2 of this study?
